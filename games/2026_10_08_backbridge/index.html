BACKBRIDGE

build forward · salvage behind

Your walker never jumps and never falls — he just stops at the edge and waits for a bridge.

Drag from any gold anchor dot to lay a plank. A plank only holds weight once both of its ends can trace a path back to solid rock, so finish the span before he can cross.

Wood is scarce: tap the middle of a plank to recycle it for 70% of its wood. Tear up the bridge behind you to build the bridge ahead.

Reach a new pillar for more time and wood. Run out of time — or drop your walker into the chasm — and it's over.
