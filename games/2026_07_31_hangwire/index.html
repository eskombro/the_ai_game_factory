HANGWIRE

Drag each incoming weight onto a hook of the hanging mobile. A beam holding 2+ weights that balances (equal turning force on both sides) chimes and clears for points.

Lopsided beams build wire strain — and so does letting the two halves of the mobile drift apart in total weight. Fill the strain bar and the wire snaps.

Drag a hung weight to move it to another hook, or drag it off the mobile to jettison it. Let 4 weights time out in the holder and you lose too.
