Valve Panic

Steam valves build pressure over time. Press and hold a valve with your finger to vent it before it hits 100% and blows. New valves come online as the shift goes on, so you'll need more than one finger. Survive the full shift with at least 1 life left to win. Venting earns points in three tiers — the riskier the save, the more it's worth.
