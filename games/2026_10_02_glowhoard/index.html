GLOWHOARD

Steal light · stay dark

You are a shy shadow-creature. Touch and hold anywhere (or click-drag) to glide toward your finger.

Grab glowing motes off the lit floor and carry them home to your dark den to bank them.

The twist: every mote you carry makes you glow. Light burns you, so the richer you are the faster you cook — but banking several motes at once is worth far more points.
