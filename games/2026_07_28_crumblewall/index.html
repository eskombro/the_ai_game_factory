Crumblewall

Creeps march from the top gate down to your keep. Build a maze to stall them and guns to kill them — but every piece rots away after a few seconds, so the maze is never finished.

  • Pick Gun or Wall, then tap or drag across the board to build.
  • Scrap It removes one of your pieces for a partial refund.
  • You can never fully seal the path — such a build is refused.
  • 5 creeps reaching the keep ends the run.
